## Terms of Use

_Last updated 4 November 2026 · version 2026-11-04_

### In short

- Kvit is a notebook for sharing expenses with friends and family. It does not hold, send or receive money.
- You must be 16 or older.
- Amounts and exchange rates can be wrong. Check them before you rely on them.
- Kvit is free and comes with no promises: it can be slow, change or stop, and data can be lost.
- What you write is yours. Be fair to other people, or your account can be suspended.

### 1. Who can use Kvit

Kvit is for people aged 16 and over. If you are younger than 16, please don't make an account or join a group.

### 2. Your account

You are responsible for everything that happens with your login. Keep your password to yourself. A guest has no email, so if a guest loses the phone or the browser, the account can't be recovered. Tell us if someone else is using your account.

### 3. A notebook, not a bank

Kvit only keeps a record of who paid and who owes. It does not hold, send or receive money, and it does not make anyone pay. “Paid” and “confirmed” mean what the people in the group say. Pay each other outside Kvit, the way you always did.

### 4. Amounts, rounding and rates

Kvit rounds amounts and changes euros and denars with the exchange rates of the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia. Rates can be late or wrong, and rounding can leave small differences. Kvit gives no financial, tax or legal advice.

### 5. How to use Kvit

Don't use Kvit to harass, threaten or cheat anyone, to share illegal content, to pretend to be someone else, to send spam, or to try to break or overload it.

### 6. What you write

The names, amounts and notes you enter stay yours. By entering them you let Kvit store them and show them to the other members of your group, so that the app can work.

### 7. Between members

Disagreements about money or anything else in a group are between its members. Kvit is not a party to them and can't settle them.

### 8. No promises

Kvit is free and offered as it is. It can be slow, stop, change or lose data, with or without warning. Keep your own copy of anything important: you can download your data in Settings.

### 9. Responsibility

As far as the law allows, the person who runs Kvit is not responsible for losses that come from using Kvit or from not being able to use it. This does not limit anything that the law does not allow to be limited.

### 10. Suspending and removing

Kvit can suspend an account or close a group that breaks these Terms or that someone reports, and can remove content. You can delete your own account at any time in Settings.

### 11. Changes

If these Terms change, you will see a notice in the app, and the date and version at the top change. For big changes we ask you to agree again. If you keep using Kvit after a change, you accept the new Terms.

### 12. Open-source parts

Kvit is built with open-source software, and each part keeps its own licence. The name Kvit and Kvit's own code are not offered for reuse.

### 13. Contact

Questions, reports and requests about these Terms or your data: write to kvit.support@gmail.com.

### 14. Law and courts

These Terms follow the law of North Macedonia, and its courts decide disputes about them. This does not take away any right that the law gives you as a consumer where you live.

### 15. If a part does not apply

If one part of these Terms is not valid, the rest still applies. Nothing in these Terms removes a right that the law gives you.
