**What Phase 15 left that matters here (verify in the code, do not trust this summary):**
- Your share of a group expense lands in your Budget through `expense_shares.counts_in_budget` (copied from the member's per-group switch when the share is saved) and is read by `PersonalEntryQueries.GroupSharesOf` / `BudgetItems`. **Goal (from the ROADMAP, DECISIONS and SCREENS; check the docs):**
1. **Possible duplicates:** when your share of a group expense lands in your budget and you already logged a personal entry in the same category with a similar amount within a few days, Kvit asks once: *"Is this the same as 'Dinner – 1,200' you added on Friday? · Same, merge · No, it's new"*. The answers are saved (`duplicate_answers`) so Kvit never asks twice about the same pair. What "merge" does is the main design question: the original idea is that the personal entry and the group share are the same money, so the budget must not count it twice (probably: the personal entry stays visible and the group share stops counting, or the other way round). Settle it with Filip before building. Guests have no Budget, so no prompt.
2. **Group totals per category:** in a group, totals per category (Hotel, Food, Transport, Other, per currency, MKD and EUR never added), visible to every member of the group. Decide with Filip where it lives (a section on the Expenses tab, a Totals tab, or a sheet).
3. **Group spending plan:** for example "600 EUR planned, 4 people = 150 each", with a progress bar and a warning near the limit (`groups.plan_amount_minor`, `groups.plan_currency`). Decide with Filip who can set it (the owner), what counts (all counted expenses of the group in the plan's currency; expenses in the other currency converted with each expense's saved rate, or ignored), what "near the limit" means (Phase 15 limit bars use green under 80 %, amber from 80 %, red when over: reuse that), and what happens in Closing and Finished groups (frozen like other group settings).
4. **Not in this phase:** future and planned payments, recurring expenses, the outbox (Release 3), admin statistics (Release 3).
5. **The phone layout comes first** (360 px, then the 64rem desktop layout), EN + MK, light and dark, glass popups for sheets, animations only transform and opacity and off under `prefers-reduced-motion`, skeletons while loading, `KvitAmount` for displayed amounts, the app's money formatter (English "MKD", Macedonian "ден."), never the word «курс» in Macedonian (use «цената на еврото»).
