1. **The texts (EN + MK) and `docs/legal/`.**
   - **Terms of Use** page `/terms` (public, like `/privacy`): short, plain, with a few-line "In short" box on top. Clauses from the research report section 4: age 16; login responsibility (guests have no email recovery); acceptable use; **notebook not a bank**; amounts, rounding and rates may be wrong, no financial, tax or legal advice; no uptime and no data-loss promise, "as is"; disputes between members are theirs; users keep ownership of what they type and Kvit may store and show it to their group; suspension and removal; changes and how people are told; contact; Macedonian law and courts without removing consumer rights; one invalid clause does not cancel the rest; a sentence that nothing removes rights the law gives.
