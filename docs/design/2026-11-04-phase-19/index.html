Side by side: what you see after confirming, and when you log in again

Option A
1. After tapping "Delete my account":
You are sent to the Welcome screen at once. A message says: "Deleted. Log in within 30 days to undo."
2. Logging in within 30 days:
You are simply in again. A message says "Deletion cancelled. Your account is back." Nothing to decide.
Good: fewest taps. Bad: the date is never shown, and a person who logs in by accident cancels the deletion without noticing.
Option B
1. After tapping "Delete my account":
A screen says: "Your account will be deleted on 4 December 2026. Nobody can sign in until then. Changed your mind? Log in."
2. Logging in within 30 days:
A screen asks "Cancel the deletion? Your account is due to be deleted on 4 December." with two buttons: "Yes, keep my account" / "No, log out".
Good: the person always knows the date and can't cancel by accident. Bad: one extra tap when changing your mind.

Why this matters: the law expects deletion within a month; tapping must switch the account off at once. "Keep my name" is preselected (your decision).
