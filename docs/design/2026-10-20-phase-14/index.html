3. Budget tab with income

Option A keeps today's card unchanged and adds a green card. Option B shows income, spent and what is left in one card. Option C changes no card; income rows sit in the list with a green "+". All three show the "Repeating income" list: Salary, 30,000 ден., monthly on the 1st, with Edit and Stop.

4. Not in this phase (my advice)

Future and planned payments (paid / not yet paid) are a different feature. I suggest keeping them in the BACKLOG and leaving the date limit as it is. Budget share, budget currency, limits and the monthly summary are Phase 15.
