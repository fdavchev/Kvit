# Phase 12: Animations + touch-ups (report, 2026-10-16)

Branch `feat/12-polish`. Dark colours, the animation pass, the glass look on every popup, the rolling amount, the small look items, and a desktop layout (phone layout unchanged). Everything was built by the coder, checked by me, then walked through in a real browser by the tester (headless, local stack).

## What Phase 12 delivered
- **Dark surfaces B:** solid hex colours instead of `color-mix` (background `#1f1816`, field `#161110`, card/sheet `#2c2220`, line `#72625c`, raised line `#8d7d77`). The phone status bar matches the page.
- **Selected pill in dark = tan `#d9b48a`** (P5, dark text `#1f1816`) on the bottom bar, the left menu, the group tabs and the Settings switches (7.99:1 against its track, 9.03:1 text). Light mode unchanged.
- **Animations (all off under `prefers-reduced-motion`):** a pill that slides under the active tab (about 180 ms), a 150 ms fade of the group tab content, toasts that pop in about 200 ms, rows that fade in and out in lists (AutoAnimate, about 200 ms, a removed row cannot be tapped), pulse skeletons instead of the spinner on Home, Groups, Members and the three group tabs, rolling digits (NumberFlow, 250 ms) on the Home totals and the group balance card. The sheet slide and the button press already existed.
- **Glass A on every popup:** sheets, dialogs, toasts (and the closing bar and Home "+" menu, which already used the shared look). 14 px blur, 90% card tint (the lowest round value that keeps text at 4.5:1 over any backdrop), bright edge. A solid fallback where `backdrop-filter` is not supported. Fields inside glass use the lighter edge so they keep 3:1.
- **Hover and rest contrast:** dark Reset 4.00 to 4.66, light Reject 4.20 to 4.86, light Object 4.11 to 4.78, light Confirm / Mark paid / Save 4.52 to 4.91; light Object and Reject text at rest 4.5:1 over any backdrop.
- **Desktop layout (64rem and wider; phone unchanged below):** a soft left menu on every signed-in screen (Home, Groups with the open groups under it, Settings); Home with huge "You are owed / You owe" numbers on the page, round action buttons, group tiles in a wrapping grid, recent activity beside; the group page with Expenses next to the balances, and a round "Add expense" button in the page instead of the floating "+" (Expenses tab only, like the "+"); the closing bar at most 40rem wide; other screens capped at 40rem.
- **Small items:** English "Accommodation" is "Stay", Macedonian «Смештај», settings theme «Тема»; the Google button frame title follows the page language; toasts no longer cover the "+"; the "Updating…" label sits at the top of the screen (phone and desktop); the Add expense screen warns the owner of a Closing group that saving cancels the closing; Macedonian currency labels say «ден.» (English MKD) on chips and the group size line; the Undo toast text is 0.875rem; "+ Add a name" moved inside the member list.

## Decisions and rejected alternatives
Moved word for word from `DECISIONS.md` (the log of the phase while it ran), plus the decisions of the last steps.

- **Dark surface colours: B (Filip, 2026-10-16),** from `docs/design/2026-10-16-phase-12/dark-colours.html`: a near-neutral charcoal with a warm tint, solid hex values instead of `color-mix`: background `#1f1816`, field `#161110`, raised (card, sheet) `#2c2220`, line `#72625c` (Filip's `#6a5a54` reached only 2.67:1 and failed the 3:1 edge rule, so it was lightened to 3.01:1), raised line `#7a6a64`. Orange stays on accents only. Rejected: A (deeper brown), C (lifted brown), keeping the `color-mix` values. Contrast is re-measured live when built.
- **Selected dark tab pill: P5 (Filip, 2026-10-16):** dusty tan `#d9b48a` with dark text `#1f1816`, replacing the orange `#b54a00` (`--tab-active` in dark). Rejected: the orange, P1 taupe, P2 sand, P3 bronze, P4 dark with orange ring.
- **Animated amount: A, rolling digits (Filip, 2026-10-16),** with NumberFlow (`@number-flow/react`, MIT), for display only (totals, balances), never for typing. Needs a jsdom test fix and a check of the "ден." label first. Rejected: B quick count, C soft swap.
- **Glass look on popups: A, full glass (Filip, 2026-10-16),** from `docs/design/2026-10-16-phase-12/glass.html`: strong blur, see-through tint, bright edge, on sheets, dialogs, the closing bar and toasts. The tint is made as opaque as needed so text stays at 4.5:1 or better over any background. Rejected: B soft glass, C solid.
- **Currency label (Filip, 2026-10-16):** English screens show "MKD", Macedonian screens show "ден." (the browser's own formatter already does this: `mk` gives `12.400,50 ден.`, `en` gives `MKD 12,400.50`). The rolling amount must keep exactly what the app's money formatter prints today. (In the app the formatter is the app's own, `formatMoney.ts`, because Chrome has no Macedonian number data; English prints `1,200 MKD`, MKD is whole denars only.)
- **Desktop layout picked, for now (Filip, 2026-10-16): the round-4 hybrid** (`docs/design/2026-10-16-phase-12/desktop4.html`): a soft left menu (Home, Groups with the group list under it, Budget, Settings); Home = huge "You are owed / You owe" numbers sitting on the page, round action buttons, a Needs you list, group tiles, recent activity (and a budget spot later); a group = a wide page with the group balance as a big number, round actions, underlined tabs, and Expenses next to Who pays whom on wide screens. Only for desktop: **the phone layout stays exactly as it is.** Filip: "satisfactory for now, we will change it later again, maybe". Rejected for now: three panes (A), list + inspector (C), centred column (E). Earlier: Filip rejected the first "sidebar + cards" mockups and wanted genuinely different ones; the research of similar apps was thin (mostly NOT VERIFIED, no screenshot was seen by the researcher). Rules: it must also make room for the budget screens of Release 2; fluid (rem, %, `clamp()`, grid `auto-fit`, container queries); the 360 px phone layout must not get worse. Mockups kept in `docs/design/2026-10-16-phase-12/` (`desktop.html` to `desktop4.html`).
- **Desktop build decisions (Filip, 2026-10-16):** (1) on desktop the floating "+" of the group page is replaced by a round "Add expense" button in the page (it covered the Everyone list); (2) the desktop breakpoint stays 64rem (a window narrower than that keeps the phone layout); (3) no "New group" button on desktop Home (Phase 11 rule: the "+" list has no New group item); (4) the left menu's group list shows only open groups, from Home's saved data, no new request; (5) desktop Expenses tab makes 3 extra requests to show balances beside it (phone makes none), accepted for family use, measure before the app grows (BACKLOG); (6) only Home and the group page are wide (64rem), every other screen is capped at 40rem. "rem" explained to Filip: a size that scales with the text size, 1rem is normally 16 px, so 64rem is about 1024 px; the switch point of a layout cannot be a percentage in CSS, and the maximum content width is a ceiling (a pure percentage would stretch across a very wide monitor).
- **Step 7 decisions (Filip, 2026-10-16):** dark field-box edge `--color-night-raised-line` is `#8d7d77` (B; Filip could not see a difference between the options, so the safe one was used; the earlier `#82726c` did not reach 3:1 over bright content). The "Updating…" label moves to the top of the screen, above the page title (A); this replaces the Phase 8b placement above the tab bar, which covered content at load. Words: "Stay", «Смештај», «Тема» (reverses Filip's Phase 4 swap of «Тема» to «Позадина»).
- **Glass leftovers (Filip, 2026-10-16): yes to both:** the dark field-box edge is lightened (see above) so it stays at 3:1 over bright content under glass, and the light danger glass buttons (Object, Reject) use the darker red text also at rest.
- **Toast speed (researcher, 2026-10-16):** Sonner has no duration setting; a CSS override of `[data-sonner-toast]` transitions (200 ms) is the only route, and Sonner's own reduced-motion rule stays in force.
- **Order (Filip, 2026-10-16): "do all the steps in a row"**; look decisions (amount, glass, desktop) still needed his pick from a mockup.
- **After the browser pass (Filip, 2026-10-16), "go fixes":** the group tabs and the Settings switches use the same tan pill in dark; all fields inside glass sheets and dialogs use the lighter edge; the dark skeleton is `#423531` (1.49:1 against the page); the closing bar is at most 40rem wide from 64rem; Macedonian currency labels on chips and the group size line say «ден.» (English MKD); the Undo toast text is 0.875rem; "+ Add a name" moved inside the member list. Not built on purpose: "Updating…" on group pages (it would need to move one level up and would only watch "who is signed in"), the tan pill inside the Split sheet.
- **Technical choices by the coder (for the record):** AutoAnimate through our own hook `useListAnimation` (the library's React hook attached twice in StrictMode and animated every change twice); NumberFlow gets the German number settings for Macedonian because Chrome has no Macedonian number data (`mk` prints the same separators as `de`), with the sign, currency symbol and label taken from the app's own formatter; the test setup fakes `matchMedia` ("reduced motion: yes") so AutoAnimate switches itself off in tests; a shared `esm-env` mock and an inline entry in `vite.config.ts` make NumberFlow work in Vitest.
