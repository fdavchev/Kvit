# Phase 8: Expenses (report, 2026-10-09)

## What Phase 8 delivered
- **Expenses:** add, edit, delete (4-second Undo toast) and **Recently deleted** for 5 days with Restore. Four splits (equal with extras, exact amounts, percentages, shares) through the Phase 3 splitter; MKD and EUR, never mixed; the exchange rate is saved with each expense. Ten built-in categories. A double tap never saves twice (`clientRequestId`).
- **Exchange rate:** the NBRM middle rate for EUR, refreshed lazily when an expense is saved and the newest rate is older than 8 hours; on failure the last rate and its date are kept and the error is logged.
- **Group screen:** an Expenses · Activity pill switch, the expense list grouped by date, a + button, layout B for an empty group, the person's Google picture in the circles (initial on a colour by joining order otherwise), and the picture also in the Home circle.
- **Activity tab and change history:** one sentence per event with who, what and when; the detail screen shows the History of an expense (old → new).
- **One bill:** a dinner or a taxi in one step (amount, people as circles, paid by, split, date, category, optional title); the group is made with it. The group's name follows the app language when no title is typed ("Bill · 7 Oct" / «Сметка · 7 окт»).
- **Two-person auto-fill:** with exactly two people in an Exact or % split, typing one box fills the other with the remainder.
- **Error codes:** a test fails when an API error code has no English or Macedonian text.
- **Macedonian without browser data:** dates, numbers and relative times are our own tables, because Chrome has no Macedonian `Intl` data.
- **Backend:** two migrations (`CategoriesAndExchangeRates`, `Expenses`, new tables only), the expenses, categories, activity and One bill API, `pictureUrl` on members and `/api/me`.
- **Code-review fixes (4):** the edit event carries its currency, two identical requests at the same moment answer 200 with one expense, a missing or all-zero `clientRequestId` is a plain 400, the amount field turns red when its text is invalid.

A Sunday returned the carried-over Friday rate; tomorrow already had a rate; a date far ahead answered 404 (so a 404 is "no rate", handled as a failure with the kept row). Not verified: a public holiday, the hour NBRM publishes, terms of use. Kvit asks for today's date in Skopje time. *Rejected:* the `...D` methods (date format unknown), `GetExchangeRates` buying and selling rates (the official rate is the middle one by our choice, NBRM names none).
- **Rate date is UTC today** (changed from "Skopje time": the Linux image may have no time-zone data, and one day of difference in a carried-over rate does not matter). A failed or empty answer is a failure, never a made-up number.

Editing or deleting a deleted expense answers 404 `EXPENSE_NOT_FOUND`; restoring checks who may (403) before the state (`EXPENSE_NOT_DELETED`, `EXPENSE_RESTORE_EXPIRED`).
