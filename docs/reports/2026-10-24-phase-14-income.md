# Phase 14: income, once and repeating (report)

_Written 2026-10-24. Branch `feat/14-income`. Release 2 ("budget"), second phase._

## What you can do now
- **Add income once:** Budget tab "+" asks "What do you add?" (Spending or Income). Income: type the amount, Save (2 taps + 1 field once the choice sheet is open). Currency starts as your last used one, the date is today (last 24 months allowed), the Note chip is the title ("Salary"). No category.
- **Repeat it:** the Repeats chip cycles Never / Monthly / Weekly (day or weekday of the start date; the 29th to 31st fall on the last day of shorter months). "Starts" is the date chip (24 months back to 1 year ahead). The "Ends" chip: Never, In 3 months, In 6 months, In 1 year (exactly 3 / 6 / 12 payments) or a picked date. A repeating income with a past start makes its past entries at once.
- **Lazy, no timer:** when the Budget tab opens (and after saving, editing or restarting) the app asks the server to catch up. Entries are made in your own time zone, never older than 24 months, never twice, never again after you delete one.
- **Repeating income row** under the month card: "Repeating income · N" with a hint. 1 or 2 incomes open a sheet, 3 or more (and none yet) open `/budget/repeating`. Each shows name, amount, rhythm and the next date; Edit and Stop ("Stopped · Undo" restarts it, within 5 days). At most 20 active per person.
- **Left this month card:** with income in a month: Left (income minus spending, per currency, red when negative) with Income and Spent under it; a currency without income shows only a Spent line; a month with no income looks exactly as in Phase 13.
- **Guests** have no income and no Budget (403 / redirect), as in Phase 13.

## Migrations (CI applies them on Neon after the merge)
- `Income`: `Income` allowed as an entry kind, `personal_entries.recurring_income_id` with a unique index on (`recurring_income_id`, `entry_date`), the new `recurring_incomes` table.
- `RecurringIncomeRules`: Monthly needs a day and Weekly a weekday; title empty or 1 to 60 characters.
- `GeneratedIncomeEntries`: `personal_entries.client_request_id` may be empty.
- `PersonalEntryOriginRules`: income never has a category; a generated entry is always income; every entry has a request id or a repeating income. These fail only if bad rows already exist (none can, the code never wrote them).

**Defaults I take until Filip says otherwise (he has not answered these):**
- Repeating dates: the start may be in the past, but entries are never written for a month older than the oldest month on the Budget strip (24 months). A start in the future is allowed up to one year ahead and its entries appear when their day comes. Income once uses the Phase 13 rule (the last 24 months up to today).
- Edit changes only the entries that come from now on (generation never goes back to a date on or before the last entry made for that income, deleted ones included; the start date can change only until the first entry exists). Stop records `stopped_at` only (the end date is not touched, which would break `ends_on >= starts_on` for a start in the future); a stopped income is hidden and generates nothing more. Restart (the Undo of Stop) works within 5 days. At most 20 active repeating incomes per person. Entries already created stay as they are. Deleting one created entry does not bring it back (the deleted row still holds that date).
- **One entry per month (monthly) or per week (weekly, weeks Sunday to Saturday) (Step 3, accepted by Filip by moving on):** a new generated entry can only fall in a period after the one that holds the last entry of that income (deleted entries count). So moving a pay day inside a month never gives a second salary in that month; the next one is the next month. `nextDate` follows the same rule. Known and not handled: switching Monthly to Weekly back-fills the weeks after the last entry. *Rejected:* "the day after the last entry" (it created a second salary after a pay day edit and failed the tester's test).
- **Restart after a gap back-fills the missed dates (Step 3, not pinned by a test):** Restart works only within 5 days, so at most one or two dates.
- **Step 3 migration `GeneratedIncomeEntries`:** `personal_entries.client_request_id` is nullable (the unique index stays), because entries made from a repeating income have no request id.
