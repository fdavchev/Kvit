# Phase 16: possible duplicates, group totals per category, group spending plan

_2026-10-31 · branch `feat/16-group-insights` · last phase of Release 2 ("budget")_

## What was asked
Three features: (1) ask once when a group share looks like a personal entry you already logged, so the money is not counted twice; (2) totals per category inside a group, visible to every member; (3) a group spending plan ("600 EUR planned, 4 people = 150 each") with a progress bar and a warning near the limit. Two Phase 15 leftovers came along (Filip, 2026-10-27): a message for an invalid limit amount, and "near the limit" in words.

## What was built
- **Possible duplicates.** A card "Is this the same?" at the top of the Budget tab, one pair at a time, "Same, merge" / "No, it's new". A merged entry stays in the list, greyed with "Merged with {expense} · {group}", and stops counting in totals, limits and the summary; the group share keeps counting. In the entry's sheet, "Not the same" saves the answer "No, it's new". Migration `DuplicateAnswers`.
- **Group totals.** A 4th group tab "Totals" (MK «Вкупно»): one card per currency (MKD then EUR, never added) with the total and the categories; "No category" last. `GET /api/groups/{id}/spending`, no migration.
- **Group plan.** The owner sets a plan (amount + MKD or EUR) from the Totals tab; everyone sees the card with "{spent} of {amount}", "{amount} each · {N} people", a bar and "On track" / "Near the plan · N left" / "N over". Other-currency expenses are converted with each expense's saved rate. Setting or removing a plan writes a line in the group's Activity. Migration `GroupPlan`.
- **Leftovers.** "Near the limit" in words on Limits rows; a message under the amount field in the limit sheet and the plan sheet; one shared progress bar for limits and the plan.
- **Code review and browser-pass fixes.** One merge per entry and per expense; editing a merged entry's amount, currency, category or date unmerges it; the plan answer carries its own member count; four equal-width group tabs with smaller labels on narrow phones; Totals rows wrap a huge amount under the name; the plan message follows the currency chip; the merged label falls back to "Merged with a group expense".

### Step 1, possible duplicates (backend, migration `DuplicateAnswers`)
- **Table** `duplicate_answers` (`id`, `user_id`, `personal_entry_id`, `expense_id`, `answer` `'Same'` or `'New'` with `ck_duplicate_answers_answer`, `created_at`); three foreign keys restrict deletes; unique (`user_id`, `personal_entry_id`, `expense_id`).
- **Endpoints:** `PUT` and `DELETE /api/personal/duplicate-answers`, body `{ entryId, expenseId, answer }` / `{ entryId, expenseId }`, 204. Guest 403 `GUEST_NOT_ALLOWED` first. PUT checks the entry (404 `PERSONAL_ENTRY_NOT_FOUND`: own, spending, not deleted), then the expense (404 `EXPENSE_NOT_FOUND`: a counted share above 0 for you, expense and group not deleted), then the answer (400 `DUPLICATE_ANSWER_INVALID`, exact text `Same` or `New`, `same` is refused). DELETE checks nothing and always answers 204. No activity or usage event. Changing an answer keeps `created_at`; the same answer again writes nothing.
- **Same moment (rejected the limits pattern):** PUT is one upsert (`INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE`), DELETE one set-based delete (`ExecuteDeleteAsync`), so Postgres settles any same-moment mix with no error and no retry. *Rejected:* the `BudgetLimitChangedMeanwhile` exception plus run-once-more wrapper: a real DELETE between a PUT's two tries is a second failure and answered 500 in 5 runs of 5.
- **Month answer:** `possibleDuplicates` (always present; 14 fields; `matchedOn` `Share` wins over `Whole`, `Whole` only when you paid) and `mergedWithExpenseId` on every entry row. Rule in `PossibleDuplicateQueries` (`MaxDaysApart = 3`, `AmountTolerancePercent = 10`, whole minor units: `|entry - ref| * 100 <= ref * 10`). Shares are read 3 days beyond the month on each side; a pair shows in the entry's month. The share rules live once in `BudgetShareQueries.CountedSharesOf`.
- **Merge effect:** one rule, `BudgetItems.CountedSpendingOf`, used by totals, converted totals, limits and the summary; computed live, so deleting the group expense un-merges and Undo merges again. The summary lists the currencies of income plus counted spending, so a merged-only currency shows no row of zeros.
- **Order:** pairs oldest first (entry date, then expense date).

### Step 2, group totals per category (backend, no migration)
- **Endpoint:** `GET /api/groups/{groupId}/spending`, its own controller. Any current member, guests included; anyone else, an unknown or a deleted group 404 `GROUP_NOT_FOUND`; Closing and Finished groups stay readable. Answer `{ currencies: [{ currency, totalMinor, categories: [{ categoryId, totalMinor }] }], plan }`; `plan` is always present and `null` until Step 3 (typed `GroupSpendingPlanResponse?`). `GET /api/groups/{id}` and the other group answers are unchanged (pinned by tests).
- **What counts:** every expense that is not deleted, its whole amount in its own currency; settlements never; MKD and EUR never added or converted. One row per currency that has expenses (MKD first, then EUR, the `Currency` list order, not text order); categories in the built-in order, "no category" last. Group expenses use built-in categories only, so custom categories cannot appear.
- **One rule for "counted":** `BalanceQueries.CountedExpenses` is now public and used by balances and totals. *Rejected:* copying the filter. Postgres does the adding (group by currency and category); the code adds categories into a currency total with overflow checks. *Test fix:* one expected total in `GetGroupSpendingLargeAmountsTests` was a copy-paste slip (...700 instead of ...600) and was corrected.

### Step 3, group spending plan (backend, migration `GroupPlan`)
- **Columns** `groups.plan_amount_minor` (bigint null), `groups.plan_currency` (text null); checks `ck_groups_plan_both_or_neither`, `ck_groups_plan_amount_minor` (above 0, at most 999 999 999 999), `ck_groups_plan_currency`, `ck_groups_plan_mkd_whole_denars`. The same migration widens `ck_activity_events_type` for `GroupPlanSet` and `GroupPlanRemoved`.
- **Endpoints** (in `GroupsController`): `PUT /api/groups/{id}/plan` `{ amountMinor, currency }` and `DELETE`, 204. Same checks and order as Update group: member (404 `GROUP_NOT_FOUND`), owner (403 `GROUP_NOT_OWNER`, a guest too), frozen (`CheckCanBeChangedBy`: Finished 400 `GROUP_FINISHED`; a Closing group stays open to its owner and the plan does not cancel the closing), then amount (400 `GROUP_PLAN_AMOUNT_INVALID`, one code for every bad amount or wrong type), then currency (the existing `GROUP_CURRENCY_INVALID`, shared with group settings through `GroupSettings.CurrencyOf`, a wrong type reaches it too), then whole denars for MKD. *Note:* so a non-owner in a Finished group gets 403, not `GROUP_FINISHED` (Filip's pick said "Finished refuses everyone"; the existing order puts the owner check first).
- **Activity:** one row only when something really changes: `GroupPlanSet` (data `{ amountMinor, currency }`) and `GroupPlanRemoved`; same value again and removing when none write nothing. No usage event. The website's activity list got the two sentences (drafts: "{name} set the plan to {amount}" / "{name} removed the plan"; MK «{{name}} постави план од {{amount}}» / «{{name}} го отстрани планот»); `approvedTexts.test.ts` lets them through as Phase 16 drafts, like Phases 9, 10 and 12b.
- **`/spending` plan:** `{ amountMinor, currency, spentMinor, perPersonMinor, overMinor, includedOtherCurrencyMinor }`. `spentMinor` = counted expenses converted to the plan currency, each on its own with its saved rate (`BudgetItems.ConvertedSumOf`, then summed); `includedOtherCurrencyMinor` = the original amounts of the converted ones; `overMinor` = max(0, spent - amount); `perPersonMinor` = amount / current members (`CurrentGroupMembers`: plain names and guests count, left and removed do not) rounded to the nearest step of the currency, halves away from zero, whole numbers (`MoneyRules/PerPersonShare`). `GET /api/groups/{id}` and the create answer gain `planAmountMinor` and `planCurrency` (18 fields); the list, members, expenses, balances and activity answers are unchanged. A deleted-then-restored group keeps its plan.
- **Same moment:** groups have no concurrency token, so two changes at once are last-write-wins (no 500; the same-moment tests repeat 20 times and passed 5 full runs). *Known limit (BACKLOG):* EF writes only the changed columns, so two same-moment changes could mix amount and currency (from 1000 EUR, one sets 1000 MKD and another 600 EUR: could end as 600 MKD). Needs two devices of the owner at the same moment; the fix (mark both columns changed) needs an `IGroupRepository` method and the test fake `FakeGroupRepository` updated. *Rejected:* adding `xmin` only for this.
- **Test fix:** `Put_NewPlan_WritesExactlyOneGroupPlanSetEvent` expected 1 activity row in total but also listed 2 types (`GroupPlanSet`, `GroupCreated`); corrected to 2.
