## BUGS
I did not fix anything. Numbered by how much they hurt.

**Bug 1 (medium): a long one-word title in the "From groups" block pushes the page wider than the screen at 320 and 360 px, so the whole Budget tab scrolls sideways.**
- Steps: have a group expense with a title that is one long word (60 letters, e.g. `Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious...xyz title`) in a month, with you in the split. Open the Budget tab at 320 or 360 px, that month.
- Expected: the title wraps (or breaks inside the word) inside the row, like the personal entries (they are clamped to two lines) and the group label (it wraps), and the page does not scroll sideways.
- Actual: the title runs out of its row and the card to the right; `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` is 576 at both 320 and 360 px (window 320 / 360). The group label under it and the amount are fine. No problem at 768, 1280, 1920. 8 of 8 combinations (320 and 360 x EN and MK x light and dark) fail; with converted total on and off.
- Cause (read in the code, not changed): `src/web/src/features/budget/overview/components/GroupShareListItem.tsx`, the `data-share-title` span (`font-semibold break-words`) has no `max-w-full`, unlike the group label span under it, which has `max-w-full` and wraps. `break-words` alone does not shrink the width a one-word text asks for in a column flex item with `items-start`.
- Screenshots: `docs/reports/screens/phase-15/bug1-long-title-overflow-360-en.png`; whole page `shots\mxB-budget-oct-320-mk-dark.png`, `shots\mxC-budget-oct-apart-360-en-light.png`.

**Bug 2 (low, keyboard and screen reader): after the first limit of a month is saved, or after "Remove limit", the focus goes to the page body instead of a button.**
- Steps: in a month without any limit, press Tab until "Set a limit" (or tap it), choose "Whole month" (or any category), type an amount, press Enter or Save. Or: open a limit row, press "Remove limit".
- Expected (DECISIONS Step 7: "the focus goes back to the row"): the focus lands on a control of the Budget screen.
- Actual: `document.activeElement` is `BODY`. The "Set a limit" row that opened the sheet is replaced by the Limits card, and the removed limit's row is gone, so there is nothing to return to. Saving a second limit (the card's own "Set a limit" link or an existing row) returns the focus correctly (BUTTON). The next Tab starts again from the top of the page (32 Tab presses to come back to "Set a limit" on the Budget tab).
- Seen with the mouse, and with the keyboard (Tab, Enter, type, Enter). Focus return on Escape works in every case (checked below).
- Screenshots: none (it is a focus value read from the page). Script `k2.py`, `l1.py`, `l5.py`.

**Bug 3 (low): a month that the server refuses (a valid-looking month outside 2000-01 to 9999-11 range on the summary address) shows "Loading..." for about 7 seconds before the error.**
- Steps: open `/budget/summary?month=9999-12` (or `1999-12`).
- Expected: the server answers 400 `PERSONAL_ENTRY_MONTH_INVALID` at once, so the screen can show "This month can't be shown. Pick another month." right away.
- Actual: the request is sent 4 times (a 400 is retried 3 times by the query), the screen says "Loading..." for about 7 seconds and then shows "This month can't be shown. Pick another month. / Try again". "Try again" cannot help. Nothing wrong is saved. A person can only get here by typing the address, so low.
- Screenshot: `shots\m3b-loading.png` (script `m3b.py`).

## OBSERVATIONS (not bugs, for Filip to decide or know)
1. **Back from the Summary goes to the current month** (DECISIONS open item 10, still open). Opened Aug > Summary > Back: URL `/budget`, the Budget tab shows Oct. Also Back is a normal link, so the browser's own Back then returns to the summary again (Budget > Summary > Back > browser Back shows the summary, a small loop).
2. **Bad `?month=` values** (`october`, `2026-13`, `2026-10-01`, `2026-1`, an empty `?month=`) show the generic "Something went wrong. Please try again. / Try again" screen with no request sent, as decided; "Try again" does not help. Missing `?month` gives the current month (Oct vs Sep). A future month (`2026-11`) works and shows everything down 100 %.
3. **Invalid limit amounts give no message in the sheet.** `0`, `12.5`, `12,5`, `1e3`, `-5`, `abc`, `0000`, `1000000000000`, `999999999999`, 23 digits: Save is simply disabled, no text says why (same behaviour as the entry amount in Phases 13 and 14, Observation 8 there). The server messages exist and work (checked with faked refusals below), but the screen never sends a bad amount. The input accepts letters and a decimal sign in the field (type=text style), only Save stays off. The largest amount the field allows is 9,999,999,999 MKD.
4. **The three bar colours differ only in colour.** Green, amber (`warning`) and red: amber `#9a5b00` and red `#b71f1f` in light are both dark warm tones; only the "N over" line separates over from near, and there is no text for "near". The near state has no words. Colour measured below passes.
5. **The new Budget radios save on every arrow press.** Arrow Down on the focused radio selects and saves at once (3 presses = 3 `PUT`s, `null`, `MKD`, `EUR`). It is how native radios work and matches "one tap saves", just noting that keyboard users send one request per step.
6. **Saving a limit from an earlier month writes this month** (as decided, the sheet says "You are looking at Aug. The change starts this month."). "Remove limit" in an earlier month's sheet also removes the limit from this month on, not that month's; the same line explains it.
7. **The Settings theme and EN/MK buttons are 38 px high** (the new Budget card's rows are 44 px). Old Settings code, not Phase 15; listed because my tap-target scan flags them.
8. **At 320 px the "From groups" group label** (outlined) wraps over 5 lines for a 60-letter group name (it breaks inside the word, as designed: "never cut").
9. A closed-then-reopened Budget tab keeps working with a `Closing` and a `Finished` group's shares (they still count and the row opens the group's expense page), which matches DECISIONS ("their shares keep counting").
