## Step 10, the code review (`/code-review high`)
It found 7 issues; I read the code for the first four and they were real.
- **Fixed** (backend, VERIFIED by automated test): (1) the "Former member" rewrite of an expense's split text used a plain text replace and also changed other names that contain the old one ("Anabel", "Dana"); it now replaces only whole entries, tested with Cyrillic, spaces and special characters. (2) "Erase name" kept the erased name in the owner-tools log; the log now shows "Former member", and a purge (erase-name or day 30) rewrites the label in that person's older log rows. (3) Erase-name and its log row are now one transaction, like suspend; if the log row cannot be saved, the purge is undone (tested). (4) A suspended person can again download their data and request deletion (the privacy notice promises both); every other call stays blocked. Extra: the backup job fails with a clear message when the passphrase is under 30 characters (the repository is public, so only the passphrase protects the 90-day artifacts).
- **Known gap:** the split text cannot be read back unambiguously; a member whose name itself contains ", " followed by the deleted person's name (for example "Bob, Ana") would have its tail replaced. Unlikely, accepted.
- **Left in BACKLOG:** silent defaults in the web client's `meService.ts`; the export leaves out groups deleted in the last 30 days; the licence generator's `npm ls` on the Cloudflare image; the log shows an admin's current display name.

## Accepted risks (Filip chose no lawyer, 2026-11-04)
The seven open questions in the research report, section 12 (imprint and address, whether the DSA or GDPR applies, the payment law, keeping shared rows after deletion, whether the agree line binds, the Agency's 15-day transfer notice, a company) stay open. Plus, from this phase: the activity history of a group keeps the text of events about removed expenses (titles and amounts inside the event text); a purge cannot change free text people typed (expense titles, notes); the export shows later states of rows from before a person left; the backup file is encrypted but not tamper-proof (openssl CBC); activity-based "active people" numbers older than 12 months become lower. **Triggers to reconsider:** strangers joining in numbers, any money feature, ads or fees, a complaint or a request from an authority.
