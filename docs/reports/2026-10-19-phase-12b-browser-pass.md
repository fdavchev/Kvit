## Bugs found
Numbered, with the severity as the tester sees it. Nothing was fixed.

**Bug 1 (medium, shown every time): a guest who taps a free name creates two identical "Marko joined" lines.** The group's Activity tab, and Home's Recent activity (seen by the guest and by the owner), show "Marko joined" twice, and one more for every guest who taps a name.
- Repro: owner makes a group, adds the plain name "Marko", copies the link. A signed-out browser opens the link and taps "Marko". Open the group's Activity tab as the owner.
- Cause (read in the code, not changed): `src/api/Kvit.Domain/Services/Groups/JoinGroup.cs` lines 74 and 75 write both a `MemberJoined` and a `MemberClaimed` event; the database confirms it (`activity_events` rows `MemberClaimed {"name":"Marko","claimedName":"Marko"}` and `MemberJoined {"name":"Marko"}` at the same instant). The Step 5 rule in `src/web/src/features/groups/activity/activityLines.ts` (lines 65 to 70 and 160 to 162) turns the same-name `MemberClaimed` into the "joined" sentence, so both read "Marko joined". A guest who types a new name has one line (correct: "Ana joined" appeared once).
- Screenshots: `shots\s2-owner-activity.png` (the owner's Activity tab, the two lines are in the text read in the run) and `shots\s4-B-home.png` (the doubled lines on Home).
- Likely fix (for the coder to decide): when the claimed name equals the account name, hide the `MemberClaimed` line instead of showing the joined text twice; or do not write the second event on the server for a guest.

**Bug 2 (low, cosmetic): the Members row of a guest who tapped a free name shows "Took the name "Marko"" under the name** (`src/web/src/features/groups/members/components/MemberListItem.tsx` lines 31 to 34). The approved mockup shows the Guest badge and nothing under it. It reads oddly because the name is the same one. A guest who typed a new name (Ana) has no line, and neither does a guest after the upgrade.
- Repro: as in Bug 1, then open the owner's Members screen. Screenshot `shots\s2-owner-members-guest.png`.

**Bug 3 (low, a very long name, already there before 12b but the new join screen shows it): a long name without spaces breaks the layout.**
- Join screen: `src/web/src/features/groups/join/components/InviteCard.tsx` line 16 (the heading has no word-breaking) and line 24 (`whitespace-nowrap` on every member name in "In the group:"). A group with a 60-character one-word group name or member name makes the page 774 px wide on a 360 px phone (checked by routing a copy of the real preview answer, 60 "W" letters). A real hyphenated name "Александра Константиновска-Папазоска Јовановска" (46 characters) already makes the page 361 px wide, so the line touches both edges. The names in the tap list wrap correctly.
- Members screen: a guest or plain name of 60 letters without spaces makes the page 1023 px wide (`MemberListItem.tsx` line 45 has `break-words`, which is not enough inside a flex row); Home's greeting "Hi, <name>" (a guest account with that name) is 2309 px wide.
- Repro: add a plain name with 60 "W" letters on the Members screen, open the invite link on a 360 px phone. Screenshots `shots\x-join-60char.png`, `shots\x-members-60char-guest.png`, `shots\v-join-names-360-en-light.png` (the 46-character name).

No other bugs were found in the guest flow. No backend bug, no wrong status code, nothing that lost a name, an expense or a balance.
