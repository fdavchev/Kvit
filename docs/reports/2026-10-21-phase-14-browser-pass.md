## API probe (direct calls from the signed-in browser session, VERIFIED by live run)

### New bugs
**Bug 7 (low to medium, accessibility): in light mode the locked date chip's text is 3.21:1, under 4.5:1.**
- Steps: in light mode at 360 px, open an entry made by a repeating income, or Edit a repeating income that has entries.
- Expected (DECISIONS Step 8: "its contrast is measured by the tester and made less faded if it is under 4.5:1"): at least 4.5:1.
- Actual: "10 Oct" / "Starts: 10 Jul" measure **3.21:1** in light (text rgb 150,132,111 on rgb 248,241,231, from the screenshot pixels). In dark they are 5.16:1 / 5.09:1. The reason line under the chips is fine (7.36:1 light, 11.89:1 dark).
- Cause (read in the code, not changed): `src/web/src/features/budget/entries/personalEntryChipLook.ts` line 6, `aria-disabled:opacity-50`.
- Screenshots: `t14\shots\u1-locked-light.png`, `u1-locked-dark.png`, `u2-startlocked-en.png`.

**Bug 8 (low): at 320 px a long euro category tile sticks out of its tile, and two of them run into each other.**
- Steps: in one month, spend €999,999,999.99 in Transport and €999,999,999.99 or more in Accommodation, then open the Budget tab at 320 px (EN or MK).
- Expected: the amount fits inside its tile. DECISIONS Step 8 named this as the rare case to check.
- Actual: at the 9 px floor (0.75 of 12 px), "€999,999,999.99" is 69.5 px wide in a 63.5 px tile, so it ends 6 px past the tile's right edge. The grid gap is also 6 px (`gap-x-1.5`), so with two such tiles the texts touch. The screenshot reads "€999,999,999.99€1,999,999,999.98". MKD tiles are fine because they wrap before "MKD". At 360 px the euro tile fits with 2 px to spare. The page does not scroll sideways.
- Cause (my guess): euro amounts have no label to wrap before, and the shrink stops at 0.75 (`fit-amount` in `src/web/src/index.css` lines 340 to 344; `KvitFitAmount.tsx`).
- Screenshots: `t14\shots\u8-two-eur-tiles-320.png`, `u8-eur-tile-zoom.png`.

**Bug 9 (medium, now visible to the person): two catch-ups at the same moment can deadlock in Postgres. The one that loses answers 500, and the person sees the "Couldn't update repeating income" toast.**
- Steps: have a repeating income with a past start and no entries yet (for example, made on another device). Then open the Budget tab in two tabs or on two devices at the same moment. Script: `t14\u12.py` runs 4 parallel `POST /api/personal/recurring-incomes/catch-up` calls from two sessions of one user, 5 rounds, each with a weekly income from 3 Jun (21 entries).
- Expected: one call creates the entries and the others answer `created: 0`, as in row 4c of the first pass.
- Actual: 5 of 20 calls answered **500** (per round: 3 of 4, 0, 0, 1, 1). The body: `InvalidOperationException: An exception has been raised that is likely due to a transient failure` → `DbUpdateException` → `PostgresException 40P01` (deadlock). The Postgres log says "deadlock detected ... while inserting index tuple ... in relation ix_personal_entries_recurring_income_id_entry_date", with each side on a single-row `INSERT INTO personal_entries`. It also happened once in the real two-tab browser test (11c), where the app showed the error toast. **No data is wrong:** SQL always had exactly one row per date.
- Cause (read in the code, not changed): only the unique-index violation (`RecurringIncomeEntryAlreadySavedException`, `src/api/Kvit.Infrastructure/Persistence/DuplicateSaving.cs` line 30) is run once more. A deadlock is not. One catch-up inserts its rows one by one, and two transactions that insert the same (income, date) pairs in different orders deadlock. My guess at why the order differs: EF orders the inserts by the new Version 7 ids, which are not in date order inside the same millisecond (NOT VERIFIED). Also, `RecurringIncomeRepository.ListActiveOfAsync` has no `ORDER BY`. Before Step 8 the person saw nothing, because the failure was only logged. Since the "a failure is shown" fix, they see an error toast for something that actually worked.
- Row 4c of the first pass (3 rounds of 8 parallel calls, no 500) probably missed this by luck.
